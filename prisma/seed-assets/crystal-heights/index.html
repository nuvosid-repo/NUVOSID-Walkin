Sample Flat Walkthrough

Crystal Heights, Basant Vihar, Udaipur · Shubh Shanti Construction. Interactive 3D model of Flat No. 2 (3 BHK, 1834 sq.ft) built from the brochure plan and specification sheet.

  • Dollhouse (start view) — whole flat with the ceiling off; drag to orbit, pinch / scroll to zoom.
  • Walk — tap a room in the list or the Walk button. WASD / arrows move, drag to look; on phone the left joystick moves, drag elsewhere to look, pinch to zoom out / in.
  • Rooms panel — every room with its size; tap to jump there.
  • Tap anything — shows the finish / material. Doors, windows, sliders, wardrobes, kitchen shutters, fridge, curtains, lights, fans and TVs all open / close / switch on tap.
  • 🔊 Hindi guide — a sales narrator introduces every room as you enter it; toggle with the speaker button.
  • People — scale figures show how each room is used; hide them from the ☰ panel.
